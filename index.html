Turn an old phone into an AI server.

Phonon runs a language model on an Android phone and serves it to your own network as an OpenAI-compatible API. No cloud, no subscription, nothing leaves the house.

or download the APK from GitHub

Android 12+ · 64-bit · 8 GB RAM for the small models

What you get

  • Your own endpoint. One address on your network, behind an API key. Chat, streaming and tool calls, same as any OpenAI API.
  • The model you choose. Five families, 1.3–2.9 GB each, downloaded in the app and checked before it is used.
  • Agents without changes. Point any OpenAI-compatible client at it. Two phones serve two tasks at once.

Fast enough to leave running

A Pixel 7 with a 2B model answers at about nine tokens a second, and holds six after fifteen minutes of continuous work — that second number is the one to plan with. It never got hot enough to slow down.

That is slower than a cloud API and faster than waiting for a person. For agent work that runs in the background, it is enough — and the marginal cost is whatever the phone did.

Three steps

  1. Install the app. Through Obtainium or the APK from GitHub.
  2. Pick a model. The app downloads it, checks its hash, and serves it.
  3. Point your agent at it. Use http://<phone>:8080/v1 with your key. Add LiteLLM if you run more than one phone.

What it needs

A 64-bit Android phone on Android 12 or newer, 8 GB of RAM for the small models, and a charger. A Pixel 7 is what we measure on; other phones are welcome, with their own numbers.

What it doesn't do yet

  • One thing at a time. One model per phone, one request in flight. A second request waits.
  • No charging control. The app can pause on low battery or heat; it cannot limit charge.
  • No TLS on the LAN. The API key protects the endpoint; traffic is plain HTTP.
  • Early software. Models are chosen and verified, but the app is new — expect rough edges.
